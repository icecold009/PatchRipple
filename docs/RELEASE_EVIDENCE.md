## Manual validation protocol

For a screen-reader trial, record browser, operating system, reader/version, date, and consent. Check landmarks and headings, file-list navigation, search/filter announcements, the named selected-file region, polite selection announcements from the list and graph, selection changes caused by filtering, inspector disclosure and return focus, warning category/severity, and exact-revision links. Record a specific confusing, duplicate, or missing announcement; keyboard/browser automation does not substitute for this observation.

For a physical-device trial, record device/model, OS/browser version, viewport, date, and consent. Open the offline report, navigate and select files, filter/search, expand warnings and the inspector, verify focus/scroll behavior, and check for horizontal overflow. Do not replace this with emulation or a resized desktop window.

Use [MAINTAINER_TRIAL_TEMPLATE.csv](MAINTAINER_TRIAL_TEMPLATE.csv) for real-repository accuracy and maintainer feedback. Do not enter synthetic results as participant evidence.
